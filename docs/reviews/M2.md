## Design notes

- **Joints are weld constraints between rigid bodies** (DEC-03), solved with the contacts by sequential impulses. Each records tension, compression, shear and torque from the impulse it needed. Past a limit it breaks, and it takes back the impulse beyond its limit, so a breaking joint only ever carries its limit.
- **Free assemblies move exactly as rigid bodies each step.** Positions and velocities rotate together about the assembly's centre of mass. Moving each part in a straight line instead lost (ω·dt)²/2 of the angular momentum every step: a welded pair slowed from 2.00 to 1.91 rad/s over 10 s. The centripetal impulse this rotation supplies is added to joint loads (exact for tree-shaped assemblies), so spinning too fast still breaks joints.
- **Parts of the same assembly don't collide with each other.**
- **Stores** add their contents' mass to their body. **Feed links** pump a resource between stores. The receiving body's velocity is adjusted to conserve momentum, and the mixing loss becomes heat. **Control links** carry channel values. Nothing physical reads them until the injectors arrive in M4; the inspector and key legend show the values.
