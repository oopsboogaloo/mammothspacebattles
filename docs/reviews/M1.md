## Physics notes worth knowing

- Restitution follows Newton's rule. The extra impulse at a point is capped so a contact can never return more energy than it absorbed in that step. Without the cap, off-centre impacts with friction gained up to 4% energy (Kane's paradox).
- Two discs that meet mid-step are solved at their time of impact, then moved onto their true paths. Oblique disc collisions match the closed form to ~1e-16. Polygons use speculative contacts with the start-of-step normal, which is standard and accurate to one step.
- The ledger attributes changes to: user commands, the gravity field (including the integrator's numerical work), contacts with fixed bodies, and position correction. A residual above tolerance is a violation. So is a contact turning heat back into kinetic energy.
