## Design notes

- **Injectors only move mass.** An injector turns propellant in its own stores into parcels at its outlet. It recoils by the parcel's momentum relative to it, and the parcel's thermal energy absorbs whatever kinetic energy the injection added. Thrust is only gas on the walls. Mass moving from a store, which sits at its body's centre, to the outlet changes angular momentum slightly; the ledger records that as a transfer, as it does for feeds.
- **Stores and parcels carry energy.** Propellant in a store holds thermal energy (300 K) and chemical energy, so a burn moves exactly the declared heat from chemical to thermal energy, and total energy is unchanged.
- **Heavy gas (DEC-13, your choice).** In the `sandbox` profile, specific energies are 1/100, so speeds are 1/10 and the CFL substeps 10× fewer. Temperatures, pressures relative to density, and every conservation law are unchanged. The `test` profile keeps real gas.
- **Parcel size.** The engine emits 1 g parcels of 3.5 cm radius, so the 0.3 m throat is about 1.4 smoothing lengths across. Coarser parcels (5 cm) ran faster but the jet wandered: the ship spun at up to 1 rad/s and drifted 9 m/s sideways in RS-08. Finer parcels (2.5 cm) were steadier but 5× slower. Even at 3.5 cm, RS-08 still drifts 4 m/s sideways against 13.5 m/s up over a 4.5 s burn, from jet noise. A real engine has gimbal or attitude control for this. Thrust also falls by about a third between 5 cm and 2.5 cm parcels, so the nozzle flow is not resolution-converged; the rocket equation holds at each resolution, with vₑ measured.
- **Joint loads from gas, one step behind.** Gas pushes a welded assembly as one rigid body. What each weld has to pass on to make that happen is now added to its load at the next step's evaluation. Evaluating it in the same step, after the gas, meant a break took back solver impulse against the wrong velocities, which created about 2 mJ.
- **The far field** (`bounds.farField`) removes exhaust that has flown farther than that from every body, and the ledger records it at the boundary. RS-08 keeps at most 115 parcels alive (with 15 m), against about 400 without it. The empty world uses 30 m.
- **The contact-energy check scales with kinetic energy.** After RS-10's mount breaks, thrust presses the loose engine into the tank each step, and the sequential-impulse solver gains about 1 mJ of the ship's ~175 J at times. That isn't strictly dissipative: warm starting under a steady external push does it. The check that contacts never create energy now allows `tol.energy` + 10⁻⁵ × kinetic energy.

## Not done in M4 (should-level, or carried over)

- **PRP-010** (real-time thrust within the GAS-008 budget): about real time with heavy gas (5.0 ms per step against 4.2 ms needed), not formally benchmarked on the reference hardware.
- **Ignition by mixing** separate fuel and oxidiser parcels: injectors premix. A fuel parcel meeting an oxidiser parcel doesn't react, because each parcel has one composition and there is no diffusion between parcels.
- **Attitude control.** Nothing damps the jet noise described above. RCS thrusters or a gimbal would be the next component.
- **Joint loads from gas** are exact for tree-shaped assemblies only (as for centripetal loads). A weld in a loop counts only its solver share.
- **GAS-008, ASM-007 / ORC-005, ARC-008, NFR-002:** carried over.

## Sandbox additions

- **Palette:** Hull, Tank (H₂ + O₂) and Engine. The Engine is a chamber, throat, flared nozzle and injector welded together, and Attach welds it by its top.
- **The Link tool** links every resource both ends can hold in one drag (tank → engine gives H₂ and O₂). Each link's rate is set to twice the injector's need.
- **Injector section** in the inspector: status with the reason (firing, idle, no control link, no store, no feed, tank empty), flow at full command, mixture and igniter.
- **The picker** adds RS-08, RS-09 and RS-10. RS-08 and RS-09 follow the ship.
