## Design notes

- **Only bodies with a material take damage.** Worlds from before M5 behave as before, including LAND and FT, where you still just bounce off.
- **Energy, not force, decides damage.** Each contact pair's lost kinetic energy (COL-006) is split equally between the two bodies. A perfectly elastic bounce does no damage.
- **Welds weaken.** A weld near a hit loses integrity, and its limits scale with it. A weld also now carries at most its limits during the solve (M4b notes), so breaking never creates energy.
- **Leaks reuse the injector's emission.** Mass, momentum and energy are handled by one function for both, `emitParcel`.
- **Armour needs nothing special.** It is just a tough material in front of the part it protects.

## Known limits

- **One breach per body.** It opens at the first hit; later hits widen it rather than adding another.
- **Store pressure is a proxy.** It is how full the store is: stores have no declared volume or temperature.
- **Gas does no damage.** Hot exhaust and explosions don't harm parts; only contacts between bodies do.
- **Damage is per body.** A big hull is damaged as a whole, not where it is struck; only its welds see where.
- **Wrecked engine walls leave the declared interior behind.** An engine's GAS-009 interior stays where it was declared.
