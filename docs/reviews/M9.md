## The ship's own shell: damage to the ship by distance

Measured 11 November 2026, at the tuning in #115. One shell fired at a still rock from 13
distances, on three rocks (39 runs), and each piece of damage traced to its cause. Distance is
from the gun's muzzle to where the shell burst.

| Burst distance | What happens to the ship | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| 0.7 m | Lost every time: all parts hit at 100%, 11 or 12 destroyed | Blast |
| 1.4 m | Lost every time: 16 parts hit, 4 destroyed | Blast |
| 2.6 m | 6 or 7 parts take up to 71%; lost on one rock of three | Blast, then flung rock and the ship's own debris |
| 3.7 m | One part takes 6%; 1 or 2 welds broken | The blast's edge, and gas |
| 4.4 to 10 m | No blast damage; 1 to 3 welds broken on nearly every shot | Gas |
| 12 to 15 m | 2 welds broken on one rock of three | Gas |
| 20 m | 1 weld broken on one rock of three | Gas |
| 25 to 30 m | Nothing broken | |

- **Inside 4 m it is the blast's declared damage.** It reaches anything whose outline is within
  4 m, fading to nothing there. It is sized for rock, and the ship's parts are 13 times weaker.
- **From 4 m to about 15 m it is gas pressure on the front thrusters.** Every weld broken in this
  range was a front thruster's: its mount to the tank (torque) or a nozzle wall (shear or
  torque). They face forward, so their nozzles catch the gas.
- **Then what follows.** A thruster torn off cuts its fuel line and its tank leaks (MAS-037). The
  loose parts sometimes strike the ship: up to 61% off a part at 5.6 m, 37% at 11.6 m.
- **Flung rock struck the ship in 1 run of 39.**
- **The bursts where a piece is destroyed (MAS-027) and the shell's extra gas each add to it.**
  With either switched off, front thrusters were still torn off, less often, and not past 10 m.
- **The shell pushes the ship back:** 2 to 5 m/s at 3 to 8 m, about 1 m/s or less from 10 m.
- **With a rock against the nose the shell does not go off:** it arms only once clear of the gun.

Chloe chose to leave the front thrusters' welds as they are (11 November 2026): firing at a rock
closer than about 15 m risks the ship.

## Design notes

- **The game lives in the world** (`world.arcade`), as Lander's does, and runs inside the step. It
  adds and removes bodies through the same operations a scenario uses, and the ledger books them
  under `boundary`.
- **Four cheats were added, each declared.** A charge that makes more gas than its shell weighs;
  a material that takes a share of a blast; a body that passes through named others; a shorter
  exhaust tail in this arena. Each is an option that only this game's scenario sets, and the
  physics page lists the ones a player can feel.
- **The shell's blast is sized for rock,** which is 67 times as tough as a gunship's metal. That one
  number explained both "the gun does nothing to the rocks" and "one shell vaporises a gunship".
- **More energy did not make a blast push harder.** Half a kilogram of gas cannot move 100 kg
  pieces. More gas did.
- **Gold weighs more than the ship.** Collecting it by touch was a collision the ship lost.
- **Lander's ship is unchanged.** The Asteroids ship has its own builder (`scripts/asteroidShip.ts`).

## Known limits

- **Rocks shatter each other.** Two rocks that meet at 2 m/s break almost every weld between
  their pieces. Chloe chose to keep this as part of the physics. It is what fills a high level
  with debris, and what takes level 10 over the step budget after a minute.
- **The ship is tougher than the rocks.** It survives ramming a whole rock at 30 m/s. The first
  design had a grazing hit on a small rock survivable with damage; now it does no damage at all.
- **The demo's pilot plays, but not well.** It collects gold on level 1 and seldom clears a level.
- **The shells' weight is on the gun.** The magazines explode but hold no shells: losing one
  loses none.
- **The battery and generators do nothing yet.** Energy use is to come.
- **A gunship's shell flies 96 m,** since shells last 1 second so that the ship's faster ones cannot
  come round the arena. Whether that changes how often gunships hit is not measured.
- **A lost thruster costs its tank 10% integrity:** that is how the leak rule gets a hole.
- **One swap setting serves both games.**
- **CI's WebKit runs the game at about 16 steps a second.** Three browser tests were changed to
  check that the game runs and fires, not how far it gets in a set time. On an iPad's Safari it
  runs well (Chloe, 11 November 2026).
- **Not measured:** how well the pilot does with the present ship beyond its first gold; rock
  orbits at the present gravity beyond 20 s; what losing a nozzle wall does to a thruster.
